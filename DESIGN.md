# Stockroom interface

Task-focused inventory and movement screens with English labels. Tailwind utilities are grouped in `assets/style.css`; individual templates can also use utilities directly.

- Ground: stone-50, with white forms and tables.
- Primary text: stone-800; secondary text: stone-600.
- Primary action and selected navigation: emerald-800; hover: emerald-900.
- Errors: red-900 on red-50; focus: amber-700 outline.
- System sans typography, tabular quantities, clear hierarchy and attached field labels.
- Shared navigation above a bounded content area. Tables scroll horizontally on narrow screens; forms become one column below the medium breakpoint.
- Screen mode: operate. Table clarity and safe stock entry take priority over decorative effects.
